Built numerical plug-flow reactor models for selective n-butane oxidation under isothermal, adiabatic and cooled operation.

- Solved material and energy balances in Python to compare conversion, yield, selectivity and temperature profiles.
- Identified how coolant temperature, pressure and feed concentration affect productivity and burnout risk.
- Recommended a controllable operating window around 380 C and about 30 m3 instead of relying on one fragile optimum.
