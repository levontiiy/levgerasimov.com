Designed an industrial TEMA BEM exchanger for orange juice pasteurisation using pressurised hot water, accounting for fouling, cleanability and pressure-drop limits.

- Sized the exchanger using the Kern method; refined shell-side performance using Bell corrections.
- Selected the tube arrangement and heat-transfer area to meet thermal requirements with a design margin.
- Checked tube- and shell-side pressure drops against design limits.
